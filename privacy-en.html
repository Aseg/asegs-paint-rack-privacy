Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-25

Who is responsible

Antonio Asenjo Soriano (asegdev). Contact for any privacy matter: aseg.developer@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Your miniatures, recipes, paints, photos, statistics and settings are stored only on your device. They leave it only if you create a copy (exporting a file or the cloud backup).

Account and cloud backup (optional)

If you create an account, we store your email address and an encrypted hash of your password to sign you in; we never store your password in plain text. If you turn on the cloud backup, we upload a copy of your app data (miniatures, recipes and paints) to the server and, if you set one, your profile photo. They are used only so that you can restore them on another device. The server is paintrack-api-production.up.railway.app, hosted on Railway in the European Union (Amsterdam, Netherlands): the API, the database and the profile photo storage are all in that region. Without an account the whole app works without sending anything.

Camera, photos and scanner

The camera is used to photograph your miniatures and to scan paint labels. Text recognition runs on your phone (Google ML Kit, offline): scanner images are not sent to any server.

Usage statistics

The app sends performance metrics, basic usage events to Expo (EAS Observe), such as “miniature added” or “backup exported”, and the errors that occur in the app. Each installation carries a random, pseudonymous identifier that does not identify you as a person and changes if you reinstall the app. They do not include the content of your miniatures, recipes or photos. Expo is a United States company, so this data may be processed outside the European Union.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your own device. We do not use server push notifications.

What we do not do

We do not sell or share your data with third parties for commercial purposes, and the app shows no ads.

Retention and deletion

Your account and cloud backup data are kept while you have the account. You can delete your account and all its data from the server inside the app: Profile → Account → Delete my account. Your user, email, cloud backup, profile photo and sessions are deleted immediately. You can also ask by writing to aseg.developer@gmail.com, and we will handle it within 30 days (more details). Data that exists only on your phone is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Your rights

You can access, correct, delete, restrict, object to and receive your data in a portable format by writing to aseg.developer@gmail.com. You may also complain to your data protection authority (in Spain, the Spanish Data Protection Agency).

Children

The app is not aimed at children under 14 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, it will be updated here with the new date.

Brand and paint names (Vallejo, Citadel, Army Painter, Scale75, AK Interactive, etc.) belong to their respective owners and are used only to identify products. This app is independent and is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by any of those brands. Catalogue colours are approximate.

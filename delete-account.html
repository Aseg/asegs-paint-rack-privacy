Aseg's Paint Rack: delete your account and data

Last updated: 2026-11-25

This page explains how to delete your Aseg's Paint Rack account and the data the server keeps. The app works without an account: if you never created one, there is nothing to delete on the server.

Option 1: from the app

  1. Open Aseg's Paint Rack and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Account → Delete my account.
  3. Confirm the deletion.

Option 2: without the app

Write from the email address you used to create the account to aseg.developer@gmail.com with the subject "Delete account". We will handle the request within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your user and email address.
  • The encrypted hash of your password.
  • The cloud backup of your data (miniatures, recipes and paints).
  • Your profile photo.
  • Your signed-in sessions.

Deletion from the app is immediate and cannot be undone.

What is not deleted

Data stored only on your phone is not on the server: it is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings. Performance metrics sent to Expo (EAS Observe) are tied to a random installation identifier, not to your account.

Aseg's Paint Rack: how to test the app before its release

Last updated: 2026-11-25

Aseg's Paint Rack is an app for miniature painters. It lets you keep in one place your miniatures (with photos and progress), your paint recipes (the paints and steps you used), your paint collection, plus statistics and reminders about your painting. The scanner reads paint labels with the camera, and recognition runs on your phone. The whole app works without an account; the account and cloud backup are optional.

I am testing the app with a group of testers before publishing it on Google Play. If you want to help, these are the steps.

Who's behind this

Hi, I'm AsegDev. I'm a developer, I hadn't painted in years and recently picked the brushes back up. I paint very rarely (Warhammer The Old World orcs, Marvel Crisis Protocol and some Warp Miniatures gnomes) and it always goes the same way: when I go back to a mini I have no idea which paints I used. So I made myself a recipe notebook.

Then I watched a YouTube video and thought: this, but like Strava, with stats on what you paint. Though I'm not sure I want to see the numbers, because the pile of shame is winning by a mile.

If you try the app, write to me about anything. I read it all myself.

What you need

  • An Android phone.
  • A Google account: the same one you use on that phone. You must use that same account in every step.

Steps

  1. Join the testers group with your Google account: https://groups.google.com/g/asegs-paint-rack-testers ("Join group" button).
  2. Open the test link with that same account and tap "Become a tester": https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.asegdev.apr
  3. Install the app from the Google Play page that link takes you to.

What I ask

  • Stay as a tester for at least 14 days in a row. Google requires it before the app can be published.
  • Use the app normally and tell me what breaks, what you miss or what is unclear.
  • Send your feedback to aseg.developer@gmail.com.

If something does not work

  • If the test link says it is not available, check that you joined the group with the same Google account you use to open the link.
  • Access can take a little while to activate after you join the group, sometimes a few hours. Open the link again later.
  • If it still does not work, write to me at the email above.

Please note

  • Test versions may contain bugs.
  • To leave the test, open the test link again and choose the option to leave the program.
  • How I handle your data is explained in the privacy policy and the terms of service. You can delete your account and data at any time from the app, or by following these instructions.

Thank you for helping improve the app!
